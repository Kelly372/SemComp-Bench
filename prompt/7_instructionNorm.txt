You are an expert visual QA assistant for outcome-driven video generation annotation.

[Input]
- Anchor image: {anchor_image}
- Video clip: {video_clip}
- Original instruction in English: "{instruction_en}"
- Original instruction in Chinese: "{instruction_zh}"

[Task]
Adjust the original instruction into concise, visually grounded, outcome-focused instructions in both English and Chinese.

The adjusted instructions should be based on:
1. the grounding elements shared by the anchor image and the video clip;
2. the core action or transformation implied by the original instruction;
3. the final visible result shown in the video clip.

[Definitions]
- Grounding elements: concrete visual objects or entities shared by the anchor image and the video clip.
- Verb: the core action or transformation.
- Result: the final visible outcome shown in the video clip.
- Adjusted instruction: a short imperative sentence describing the desired outcome.

[Rules]
- Use the original instructions to identify the intended action or transformation.
- Use the anchor image and video clip to keep only visually grounded and verifiable content.
- Preserve the core visual goal if it is supported by the image and video.
- Remove unnecessary details, fine-grained attributes, subjective descriptions, background information, and intermediate steps.
- Prefer generic visual nouns over specific names, brands, product models, or person names.
- If some details in the original instruction are not visually supported, omit them.
- If the video shows only part of the original instruction, rewrite the instruction conservatively based on the visible result.
- If the video result is inconsistent with the original instruction, still provide the best concise adjusted instructions, but assign a low alignment score.
- The English and Chinese adjusted instructions must have the same meaning.
- Do not add details in one language that are absent from the other.

The adjusted English instruction should preferably be under 12 words.
The adjusted Chinese instruction should be a short natural imperative sentence.

Alignment score:
- 3 = Strong alignment: the original instruction, anchor image, and video clip clearly support the same grounding elements, action, and result.
- 2 = Partial alignment: the main grounding or action is plausible, but some details are missing, ambiguous, or only partially supported.
- 1 = Weak alignment: the original instruction, image, and video are weakly related, inconsistent, or the action/result is unclear.

Return exactly one JSON object and nothing else:

{
  "grounding_elements": ["..."],
  "verb": "...",
  "result": "...",
  "adjusted_instruction_en": "...",
  "adjusted_instruction_zh": "...",
  "alignment_score": 3,
  "reason": "..."
}
