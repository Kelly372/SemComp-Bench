[Task]
Given the video title (`{title}`) and the image-grid sampled from the video, classify the video into one domain and one category.

Do not force every video into a listed domain. A video should be assigned to a valid domain and category only if the sampled frames provide clear visual evidence that it strictly matches the definitions below.

If the video fails the validity requirement, does not strictly match exactly one listed category, or you would otherwise need to guess or stretch definitions, you must classify it as Uncertain with category N/A. There is no separate rejection bucket: all disallowed or low-confidence cases use Uncertain.

[Validity Requirement]
We only keep videos with a clear start-to-outcome visual structure.

A valid video should show at least two of the following in the sampled frames:
1. an initial state, such as raw materials, an unfinished object, a packaged item, an unclean tool, or an unprepared subject;
2. a visible operation or transformation, such as cooking, assembling, cleaning, styling, repairing, arranging, shaping, or editing;
3. a final outcome, such as a completed product, changed state, revealed object, cleaned tool, prepared gear, finished artwork, or reorganized space.

Base the classification primarily on the image grid. The title is only supporting information and must not override the visual evidence.

Classify as Uncertain if:
- the video lacks a clear start-to-outcome visual structure;
- the video is mainly talking, reviewing, commentary, vlog, entertainment, or pure documentation;
- the video only shows continuous motion, sports action, performance, or pet behavior without a clear resulting state;
- the video is blurry, incomplete, ambiguous, or difficult to assign to exactly one category;
- the video is related to a listed domain but does not strictly satisfy any defined category.

[Classification Taxonomy]

Food and Cooking
- Dish Making: A complete dish or food product is made from ingredients or incomplete food components.
- Food Transformation: The same food item undergoes a visible cooking-related state change.
- Food Plating: Existing food elements are rearranged or decorated into a more complete presentation.

Beauty and Fashion
- Styling: The appearance of the same person is visibly restyled, such as makeup, hairstyle, or outfit change.
- Tool Cleaning: A beauty or fashion tool is cleaned into a reusable condition.
- Try-on: An external appearance reference is applied to a subject.
- Product Reveal: A previously concealed product becomes visible.

Sports and Fitness
- Gear Setup: Sports gear or equipment is configured into a ready-to-use state.
- Body Preparation: The subject enters a sports-related prepared state through warm-up, wearing gear, or adjustment.
- Sports Edits: Event identity cues are turned into an edited sports media product, such as a highlight, trailer, or recap.

Crafts and DIY
- Assembly: Separate parts are combined into a complete structure or object.
- Restoration: The condition of the same object is restored or refurbished.
- Renovation: A built space is reorganized or remodeled into a new spatial state.
- Blueprint Construction: A physical structure is built according to an abstract design specification.
- Material Shaping: A raw material is physically reshaped into a new form.

Gardening and Pets
- Plant Treatment: A plant is treated through care, propagation, transplanting, or recovery-oriented operations.
- Floral Design: Floral or plant elements are trimmed and arranged into an aesthetic design.
- Pet Grooming: A pet’s appearance is improved through grooming.

Arts and Precision
- Artwork Creation: An artwork is completed from an unfinished visual basis.
- Digital Creation: A digital creative product is completed from a visual or abstract reference.
- Sculpting: A sculptable material is turned into a finished 3D artwork.
